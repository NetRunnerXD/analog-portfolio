## 2. Required project page fields

PROVISIONAL (2025). The 2026 form will be described in the Nov 13 guide. Based on the 2025 official guidance and walkthrough video ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SAm_2GB7PGc)), the form includes:

- Project name
- High-level project summary
- Link to project demo (one link only, in "Demonstrate your solution")
- Link to final project / public code repository (if applicable)
- Detailed project description
- Use of Artificial Intelligence (disclosure of tools and how they were used)
- NASA data and resources used (links)
- Space Agency Partner data and resources (links, optional)
- Other references / resources (links), including licenses for non-NASA material
- Agreement to the Participant Terms & Conditions, then **Submit for Judging**

CONFIRMED for 2026 ([Participant Terms & Conditions](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/), updated Aug 7, 2026): the submission form prompts for references, every source must be cited, and licenses for non-NASA materials must be documented (see section 4).

Older official guidance (still useful, PROVISIONAL): the project page and linked materials must be in **English**; a demo video in another language needs **English subtitles** ([2020 Project Submission Guide PDF](https://2020.spaceappschallenge.org/assets/documents/460/Space_Apps_2020_Project_Submission_Guide.pdf)).

Workflow notes (PROVISIONAL, 2025 video): the Project tab only appears once the hackathon begins; edits are private to the team until **Submit for Judging**; there's a lock toggle to avoid simultaneous edits, and Save Progress can be used repeatedly.

## 4. AI use and data use

### AI use
- The **2026 Terms & Conditions contain no AI clause** (checked [legal page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/), Aug 7, 2026 version). The 2026 AI rules will be in the Nov 13 guide. → UNCONFIRMED for 2026.
- 2025 rules (PROVISIONAL, official sources: [2025 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SAm_2GB7PGc), [nasa/spaceapps #1273](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1273)):
  - AI tools are **permitted**, but you must **disclose every AI tool** and where/how it was used.
  - AI-generated **images and video** must carry a visible watermark (e.g. "AI-generated").
  - AI-generated **code, text, data, and audio** must be acknowledged in the project description (and metadata where possible).
  - AI content must not use or modify **NASA branding** (logos, flags, mission identifiers).
- Context: NASA's own policy requires AI-generated media to be watermarked and carry metadata ([NID 1383.155](https://nodis3.gsfc.nasa.gov/OPD_Docs/NID_1383_155_.pdf)).
- **For us:** keep a running log of AI tools used (coding assistants, image generators, LLM research) from day one so the disclosure field is easy.

### Data use and attribution (CONFIRMED, [2026 T&C](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/))
- **Cite every source**, even open-source or freely available ones. NASA can remove material, nominations, awards, or winner status for missing citations.
- Non-NASA / non-partner data or code must be **freely and publicly available** or **OSI-licensed**, and the license must be **listed** in the submission. NASA won't post solutions missing license documentation.
- Original team content is released as open source; if no OSI license is specified, NASA distributes it under **Apache 2.0**.
- No export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) information, no proprietary info, no PII, no promotion of commercial products.
- Solutions must **directly address** a 2026 challenge statement.
- PROVISIONAL (2025/2020 guidance): using and linking **at least one NASA (or partner agency) dataset or resource** is expected for Global Award eligibility, and the page should clearly show how it was integrated.
- NASA name/logo: don't imply NASA endorsement; follow the [Brand Guidelines](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/brand/) (UNCONFIRMED for exact 2026 participant wording; the Navigator T&C contains this rule).

## 5. Judging criteria and award categories

### Process (PROVISIONAL, from [2025 Awards page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/))
1. Local Event / Universal Event judges pick **Global Nominees** (Local Lead deadline 2026: Nov 22, per a Local Event page, UNCONFIRMED on the official site).
2. NASA and Space Agency Partner SMEs pick **Global Finalists** and **Honorable Mentions**.
3. An executive committee picks **10 Global Winners**.
- Universal Event projects are only compared against other Universal Event projects (CONFIRMED, [FAQs](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/)).

### Criteria
UNCONFIRMED for 2026. 2025 guidance (PROVISIONAL, [2025 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SAm_2GB7PGc)) judged on **Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, and Presentation**. Verify on Nov 13.

### Award categories (2025 list, PROVISIONAL; [2025 Awards page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/))
| Award | Description |
|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | Best and most valid use of science / the scientific method |
| Best Use of Data | Best makes space data accessible or uses it in a unique application |
| Best Use of Technology | Most innovative use of technology |
| Galactic Impact | Most potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe |
| Best Mission Concept | Most plausible concept and design |
| Most Inspirational | Captures our hearts |
| Best Use of Storytelling | Most creatively communicates open data through storytelling |
| Global Connection | Best connects people around the world through technology |
| Art & Technology | Best combines technical and creative skills |
| Local Impact | Greatest potential for local impact |

Best fits for our project: **Best Use of Science**, **Best Use of Data**, **Best Mission Concept**. 2026 prizes/recognition: "More information will be announced at a later date" (CONFIRMED, FAQs).
